💧 Smart Life Webhook Guide

Follow these 3 easy steps to connect this app to your Smart Life sprinkler valve:

STEP 1

Create a Tap-to-Run Scene in Smart Life

  1. Open the Smart Life app on your phone.
  2. Go to Scene $\rightarrow$ Tap "+" $\rightarrow$ Select "Launch Tap-to-Run".
  3. Under Task: Add Run the device $\rightarrow$ Select your water valve $\rightarrow$ Turn ON.
  4. Add another task: Delay $\rightarrow$ Set to 8 seconds.
  5. Add final task: Select water valve $\rightarrow$ Turn OFF.
  6. Save the scene and name it "Sprinkler Blast".
STEP 2

Connect Smart Life to IFTTT (Free Webhook)

  1. Go to ifttt.com (or open the IFTTT mobile app).
  2. Tap Create (New Applet).
  3. If This: Search for Webhooks $\rightarrow$ Select "Receive a web request" $\rightarrow$ Event Name: cat_detected.
  4. Then That: Search for Smart Life $\rightarrow$ Select "Activate Scene" $\rightarrow$ Choose your Sprinkler Blast scene.
  5. Tap Finish.
STEP 3

Get & Paste Your Webhook URL

  1. Visit ifttt.com/maker_webhooks/settings to view your key.
  2. Your webhook URL will look like this:
https://maker.ifttt.com/trigger/cat_detected/with/key/YOUR_KEY

Paste this URL into the Smart Life Webhook URL field below. The app will automatically remember it!
