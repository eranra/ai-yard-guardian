METHOD 1: PHONE APP (MacroDroid - 100% Free)

Control Smart Life right from the same Android phone:

  1. First, inside Smart Life, create a Tap-to-Run scene called "Sprinkler Blast" (Valve ON $\rightarrow$ Delay 8s $\rightarrow$ Valve OFF).

  3. In MacroDroid, tap Add Macro:

    • Action: Apps $\rightarrow$ Open Shortcuts $\rightarrow$ Select Smart Life $\rightarrow$ Choose your Sprinkler Blast scene!
  4. Save the macro. MacroDroid displays your unique free Webhook URL:
https://trigger.macrodroid.com/YOUR_DEVICE_ID/cat_detected

Paste this URL below. Whenever a cat is detected, MacroDroid triggers your Smart Life valve directly on the phone!

METHOD 2: FREE CLOUD BRIDGE (SEQUEmatic)

Zero phone apps needed — Free cloud connection:

    • Trigger: Custom Webhook $\rightarrow$ Event: cat_detected.
    • Action: Tuya / Smart Life $\rightarrow$ Select your valve $\rightarrow$ Turn ON $\rightarrow$ Pause 8s $\rightarrow$ Turn OFF.
  4. Copy your generated sequence webhook URL:
https://sequematic.com/trigger-custom-webhook/USER_ID/SECRET/cat_detected

Paste this URL below. No subscription, no credit card required.
